# Driver Companion App

## Project Charter

### Mission

To create a professional companion application that assists taxi drivers by reducing administration, improving record keeping and providing reliable operational tools.

---

## Objectives

- Reduce manual data entry.
- Automate repetitive tasks.
- Maintain accurate financial records.
- Assist drivers without replacing their judgement.
- Keep the application simple and reliable.

---

## Principles

- Driver first.
- Automation second.
- Transparency always.
- Never guess financial information.
- Every automated action should be traceable.

---

## Long-term Vision

Driver Companion should become a modular platform made up of specialised assistants that help taxi drivers throughout their working day.

Examples include:

- Finance
- Flights
- Events
- Weather
- Navigation
- Vehicle Maintenance

Each module should operate independently while sharing common project standards.
